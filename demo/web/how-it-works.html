● 10 m OBSERVATION TIER

Measured Sentinel-2 reflectance. The parent NDVI drop is computed here and is the only place a change can be called observed.

■ 2.5 m MODEL TIER

SEN2SR-lite reconstruction. It supplies where inside a 10 m pixel the change may be. It is a model output, not a measurement.

▲ GATE

A 2.5 m change counts as OBSERVED only if it exceeds k·σ and its 10 m parent shows change. Anything else is labelled INFERRED or UNSUPPORTED.
