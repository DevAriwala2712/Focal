# Research landscape and dataset fit

Checked 2026-09-29 against primary project, paper and publisher pages. This is a design comparison, not a benchmark. Download and licence claims remain at the evidence level stated in [RISK_REPORT.md](../RISK_REPORT.md).

## What already works, and where TrustSR fits

| Existing work | Verified contribution | Boundary for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ESA OpenSR / SEN2SR](https://github.com/ESAOpenSR/SEN2SR) | A pretrained route from Sentinel-2 to 2.5 m, with a low-frequency hard constraint and published loading/inference examples. Its [project description](https://cin.philab.esa.int/databases/projects/towards-explainable-ai-application-to-trustworthy-super-resolution-opensr-robust-accountable-super-resolution-for-sentinel-2-and-beyond) explicitly addresses spectral consistency and trustworthy SR. | TrustSR is **not** the first trustworthy SR model. Its proposed contribution is an application-level change gate that labels how a downstream change is supported, plus a date/mask/provenance audit. The pinned lite checkpoint has a measured non-native tile-shape failure in this repository. |
| [OpenSR latent diffusion model](https://github.com/ESAOpenSR/opensr-model) | Published model and notebooks include multiple-sample uncertainty estimation. The [ESA project page](https://cin.philab.esa.int/databases/projects/towards-explainable-ai-application-to-trustworthy-super-resolution-opensr-robust-accountable-super-resolution-for-sentinel-2-and-beyond) describes sample-based pixel uncertainty and its extra compute cost. | Eight deterministic dihedral transforms of SEN2SR-lite measure transform sensitivity, **not** posterior uncertainty or a calibrated confidence interval. The low-memory target and pinned-model requirement motivate the lite path. |
| [OpenSR-test](https://github.com/ESAOpenSR/opensr-test) | Benchmark groups consistency, synthesis and correctness, beyond a single PSNR/SSIM score. Its SPOT set is a relevant ×4 RGBN reference. | Use it if installable, but keep masked PSNR/SSIM, 10 m downsample error and landslide-task evaluation explicit. Its image-quality metrics do not certify Wayanad damage labels. |
| [WorldStrat](https://worldstrat.github.io/) | Temporally matched Sentinel-2 and 1.5 m SPOT imagery across diverse land cover. | Four-band HR/LR pair preparation needs verified band order, dates, geometry and radiometry. The current three prepared train AOIs are smoke-test inputs with empirically harmonized SPOT DN, not a held-out accuracy result. |
| [Monopoli, Montello and Rossi (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20161) | Bi-temporal Sentinel-2 landslide mapping with an inventory geodatabase; their reported architecture also uses DEM. | TrustSR's four-band-only constraint excludes DEM as an input. Its downloaded Colombia polygon/crop sample proves access, but one selected positive crop cannot calibrate `k` or estimate generalization. |
| [GLaD4CD Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/10800338) | Publisher describes pre/post Sentinel-2 GeoTIFF patches in A/B folders, change labels in LABEL, a 174-event inventory CSV and 17 validation pairs. Files are listed as a 2.0 GB ZIP and a 15.7 kB CSV. | **Promising untested calibration source.** The record lists B02/B03/B04/B08 plus CLM; CLM may be a quality mask, never a fifth SR spectral band. Inspect actual ZIP members, licence, mask meaning, georeferencing, event split and class balance before use. A landing page and listed download do not prove a successful download or usable labels. |
| [Landslide4Sense official project](https://github.com/iarai/Landslide4Sense-2022) | Single-date pixel-wise landslide segmentation image/mask pairs; this repository verified a small IBM-NASA mirrored sample. | No pre/post change labels or independent 2.5 m truth. Useful for a separate segmentation comparison, not as a direct temporal calibration replacement. |

## Differentiation that can survive review

The proposed wedge is **traceable downstream interpretation**, not a novel SR backbone or the first uncertainty map. TrustSR makes an SR-only vegetation drop visible as **UNSUPPORTED** and excludes it from reported change, while marking parent-supported but spatially uncertain change as **INFERRED**. A jury can inspect the input dates, 10 m parent signal, class rule and unsupported count. This claim becomes evidence-backed only after calibration and held-out comparison; the current repository has no such result.

The honest baselines are the original 10 m change rule, deterministic interpolation and ungated 2.5 m SR, evaluated on a common labelled grid. Compare precision, recall and F1 with the same valid mask and event split. Report geospatial and spectral consistency independently. A 10 m inventory raster enlarged to 2.5 m is still 10 m evidence.

## Data qualification before calibration

GLaD4CD is the clearest new lead for packaged bi-temporal labels. Before spending 2.0 GB of disk, verify the current record/version, explicit reuse terms, checksums and a small file-list/sample access path. If the source cannot be downloaded or its label semantics do not match an NDVI-drop detector, preserve that negative finding and use the already verified inventory route with multiple held-out events. Do not alter R4's recorded 2026-09-26 outcome retroactively; add a dated continuation.

The Wayanad demo remains a separate transfer case. Its accepted December post-image and seasonally distant pre-images are a substantial confounder. Even a strong score elsewhere would not make a rapid-response or building-damage claim valid for that scene.
